Our sterilization routine, step by step

Every patient gets freshly sterilized instruments, opened in front of them. This is the process behind each appointment.

  1. 1

    Ultrasonic cleaning

    Dental burs and delicate instruments go through an ultrasonic bath that lifts away microscopic debris.

  2. 2

    Individual pouch sealing

    Each patient's instruments are sealed in their own sterile pouch with a medical heat sealer before treatment.

  3. 3

    Medical autoclaving

    High-pressure steam sterilization in the autoclave destroys bacteria, viruses and spores.

  4. 4

    Opened in front of you

    Instruments stay sealed until the pouch is opened chairside, in front of you, so you can see they are sterile.
